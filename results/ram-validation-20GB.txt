20 GB RAM Validation
====================

System:
ASUS VivoBook X415EA
Intel Core i3-1115G4

Installed memory:
- 4 GB Samsung onboard DDR4-3200
- 16 GB Crucial CT16G4SFRA32A.C8FB DDR4-3200
- Total installed: 20 GB

Test 1 - memtester
------------------

Command:

sudo memtester 12G 2

Result:

- 12 GiB tested
- 2 passes completed
- 0 errors

Test 2 - Memtest86+
-------------------

Version:
Memtest86+ v7.20

Result:

- 19.7 GB memory tested
- 4 complete passes
- Runtime: 4:28:45
- Errors: 0

During the test:
- All 4 logical CPU threads were used.
- CPU temperature reached approximately 94°C.
- The system completed the test without errors or thermal shutdown.

Conclusion
----------

The 20 GB RAM configuration passed both the Linux-level
memtester test and the boot-level Memtest86+ test.

The RAM upgrade is considered successfully validated.
