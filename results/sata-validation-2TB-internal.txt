2 TB SATA SSD Validation
========================

System:
ASUS VivoBook X415EA
Intel Core i3-1115G4

Drive:
Crucial BX500 CT2000BX500SSD1
Capacity: 2,000,398,934,016 bytes [2.00 TB]
Firmware: M6CR091

Installation:
- 2.5-inch SATA SSD installed in the laptop's internal SATA bay
- ASUS HDD FFC cable
- FFC cable part number: 04022-00080000

Connection validation
---------------------

Linux identifies the drive as:

CT2000BX500SSD1
Transport: SATA

Kernel reported:

SATA link up 6.0 Gbps

The drive was therefore successfully detected through
the laptop's internal SATA interface rather than the
previous SATA-to-USB adapter.

SMART reported:

SATA Version: SATA 3.3, 6.0 Gb/s
Current link speed: 6.0 Gb/s

SMART health:
PASSED

Selected SMART results:

- Reported uncorrectable errors: 0
- Current pending ECC errors: 0
- Offline uncorrectable errors: 0
- UDMA CRC errors: 0
- SATA interface downshift: 0

Temperature:
30°C at time of SMART check
Recorded SMART temperature range: 26°C to 41°C

Performance test
----------------

Command:

sudo hdparm -t --direct /dev/sda

Result:

1442 MB in 3.00 seconds
480.58 MB/sec

Real-world validation
---------------------

The SSD was detected successfully by Windows 11
through the internal SATA connection.

The SSD was also detected successfully by Debian.

A video stored on the BX500 was played successfully
from the drive.

Conclusion
----------

The 2 TB Crucial BX500 has been successfully installed
and validated through the ASUS X415EA's internal SATA
connection.

The internal SATA link negotiated at 6.0 Gb/s and the
drive achieved a sequential direct-read result of
480.58 MB/sec.

No SATA transmission errors or link downshifts were
reported during validation.

No further stress testing was considered necessary.
